About

Building with a QA mindset

I've always been drawn to technology — whether tinkering with computers offline or exploring fantasy worlds online. Through that curiosity I discovered a passion not just for testing, but for building.

That path led me to game development and quality assurance, where I spent over five years sharpening my eye for detail and ensuring software reliability and performance.
